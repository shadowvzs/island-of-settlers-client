tail, dark-tipped bill), simplified to about 3,000 triangles (High) and 1,500 (Minimal), its
texture baked into vertex colours (no texture shipped), one wing beat, the glide and two banked
glides from its flight animation baked into posed frames, turned upright and scaled to island
units (tools/assets/eagle-model.ts).
