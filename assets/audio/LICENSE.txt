Harbour Lanterns
Original composition and synthesized recording for Island of Settlers, 2026-10-15.
Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
No third-party music, audio samples, recordings or soundfonts are used.
The original score and reproducible synthesizer are in tools/assets/medieval-music.py.

MIDI collection renders (assets/audio/midi/)
88 compositions supplied by the project owner from resources/midi, in nine collections:
Roman, Viking, Japanese, Nubian, Chinese, Persian, Maya and Indigenous (10 each) and
neutral (8). Original source MIDI files stay outside the
game checkout. The supplied compositions are not covered by the Harbour Lanterns
CC0 dedication above. Source hashes are recorded in midi/catalog.json.

Rendered offline using FluidSynth and the FluidR3 GM soundfont.
FluidR3 GM copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen; (c) 2008 Toby Smithe.
FluidR3 GM is distributed under the MIT licence; see midi/FLUIDR3-LICENSE.txt.
The soundfont and MIDI synthesizer are not included in the browser runtime.
Reproduction pipeline: tools/audio/render-midi.py.

Garrison trumpet (assets/audio/sfx/garrison-first-occupation.ogg)
Original three-note cue written for Island of Settlers, rendered offline with the same
FluidSynth / FluidR3 GM pipeline (tools/audio/render-occupation-trumpet.py).

Every shipped audio file is listed in the game's content catalog (sound and music entries
of island-of-settlers-shared/src/catalog/data); all other game sounds are synthesized at
run time by original code and use no samples.
